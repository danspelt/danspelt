## Dan Spelt

Full-stack developer in Victoria, BC. 18+ years building web applications.

Most of my work starts with a process that takes too long: orders retyped from email into a
spreadsheet, bookings that need a phone call, a form someone prints, fills in and scans back.
I look at how the work actually happens, decide whether software would pay for itself, and if
it would, build something that fits the way the business already runs.
